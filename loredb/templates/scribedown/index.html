What is Scribedown?

Scribedown is a worldbuilding and lore management tool designed to help writers, game masters, and creators organize and develop their fictional worlds. It provides a structured way to create, manage, and explore various elements of a fictional universe, such as characters, locations, events, and more.

Features

  • World Creation: Create and manage multiple worlds, each with its own unique settings and lore.
  • Element Management: Organize characters, locations, events, items, and other elements of your fictional worlds.
  • Relationships: Define relationships between characters, locations, and other elements to create a rich and interconnected lore.
  • Wiki Integration: Create a wiki for your world to share your lore with others and collaborate on worldbuilding projects, or just for personal reference.
  • Community Features: Connect with other worldbuilders, share your creations, and get feedback from the community.

Getting Started

To get started with Scribedown, you can create a new world and start adding elements to it. Use the navigation bar to access different sections of the app, such as your worlds, wiki, and community features.

If you can't see the Elements and Wiki sections, make sure you are logged in.

The app is free to use, without subscriptions or hidden fees.

Feedback and Support

If you have any questions or feedback about Scribedown, feel free to reach out to the development team or participate in the community discussions.

About the Developer

Scribedown was created by Creestof, a worldbuilder fed up with subscriptions-based hierarchy in worldbuilding tools on the market.

His goal is to give people a free alternative containing a multitude of features useful for passionate worldbuilders, writers, creators, or even those who are just starting out.
